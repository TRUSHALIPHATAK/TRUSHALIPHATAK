
Hi, I'm **Trushali Phatak (VU3DUN)**, an aspiring **Space Systems Engineer** passionate about **RF and satellite communications, antenna systems, ground stations, embedded systems, and AI/ML applications for space**.

🛰️ My work involves the development of **satellite communication and ground station systems**. I have experience in developing an **automated ground station for satellite tracking and image decoding**, including a **QFH antenna for NOAA satellite reception**, SDR-based systems, and RF signal chains.

📡 I am currently working on **SomaiyaSat, a 2P PocketQube nanosatellite**, contributing to its communication and payload systems. My work includes **TT&C using the M17 protocol, an SSTV payload based on Robot36, RF signal-chain development, antenna systems, and deployment mechanisms**. I have also previously worked on the development of a **dual-band canted turnstile antenna (401 MHz and 435 MHz, RHCP)** for satellite applications.

📻 As an **Amateur Radio Operator (VU3DUN)**, I actively explore **HF radio communication and field operations**, gaining practical experience with real-world RF systems and radio communication.

🤖 Alongside space systems, I explore **AI/ML for space applications**, including **solar forecasting and nowcasting using Aditya-L1 data** and **event-based vision for space-object detection and maritime surveillance.**

I am particularly interested in building systems at the intersection of:
